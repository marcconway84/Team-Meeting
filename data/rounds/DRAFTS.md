# Thirty days of drafts

23 September to 22 October 2026. The 28th is already built; the other twenty-nine
are waiting on you.

## How to read this

**These are drafted from memory, not from a source.** Obscure "National X Day"
trivia and exact birth years are where I am least reliable, so every line carries a
mark:

| Mark | Means |
| --- | --- |
| **solid** | Well established. I would be surprised to be wrong. |
| **check** | I believe it, but confirm before it goes in front of anybody. |
| **shaky** | Plausible, probably invented by a greetings-card company. Cut freely. |

Some are American and may mean nothing to a team here. Marked **(US)** so they are
easy to strike out.

## What to send back

Cross things out, add anything you know, and tell me which day to build. A round
wants eight to fourteen items - enough to fill a picture, few enough to draw well.
A birthday or two each is what stops every day being a list of awareness ribbons.
